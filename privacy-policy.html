Privacy Policy

Kabur Dari Mantan

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Kabur Dari Mantan ("we", "our", or "the game") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you play Kabur Dari Mantan.

1. Information We Collect

The game does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The game does not include developer-operated analytics or advertising services.

2. Local Game Data

Game settings, scores, and progress may be stored locally on your device to support gameplay. This information is not sent to or stored on servers operated by us.

3. How Data Is Used

Locally stored information is used solely to maintain game progress, remember preferences, and enable game functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not currently integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or sign-in services into the game. Your device's operating system and the platform from which you download the game may independently process information under their own privacy policies. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit locally stored gameplay information to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Depending on your device settings, operating-system backup services may retain copies independently of us.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game does not require registration or transmit gameplay data to our servers, we do not maintain personal profiles of players. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

8. Security

Because gameplay data is stored locally, device-level security helps protect that information. No method of storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
